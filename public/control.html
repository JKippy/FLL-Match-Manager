Sounds & volume

Nature (default): gentle marimba + birdsong start, wind chimes at 0:30, bell trio + ocean swell at 0:00, soft descending tones on abort. Classic: FIRST-style charge, whistle, buzzer, foghorn. Field breaks never make a sound. FIRST doesn't publish its field sounds, so both packs are synthesized. Upload your own (mp3/wav/m4a/ogg, ≤ 8 MB) per cue; the files are stored on the laptop and sent to every screen. “Test” plays on all screens, “Here” only on this device.

Table referees

Each table referee opens their table's link on a phone (scan the code), taps READY when the table and team are set, and can tap Call Head Ref. The ready check starts once a referee page has been opened. No PIN needed on the referee page; it can only mark its own table and call you.

Table pairs

Which tables each pair screen covers. Matches are assigned to pairs from the tables in the schedule (A+B together, then C, etc. comes straight from the schedule).
